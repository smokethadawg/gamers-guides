The Map — Schematic

Liberty Falls is a small West Virginia town. You spawn on the rooftop above Pump & Pay in the south, and the Dark Aether Church with Pack-a-Punch sits at the top of the hill in the north. The bank rooftop — The Alamo — is the zipline hub that ties the whole town together. This is a schematic layout (zones in their real relative positions, not to scale). Main Quest — “Bye-Bye, Dark Aether”

The full story quest, in order. Rewards: the Bye-Bye, Dark Aether achievement/trophy, 5,000 XP, the Liberty Falls Quest Complete calling card, and the Ray Gun PJ-SRU skin. You can do it solo or in co-op, in Standard or Directed Mode. Do the Jet Gun build first (next section) — almost every step after that needs it.

Jet Gun & LTG — The Two Builds

The Thrustodyne M23 Jet Gun is Liberty Falls' Wonder Weapon. Its suction (hold primary fire) pulls in loot, power-ups, LTG parts and Aetherella figures; its blast (secondary) shoves crowds back. You can pull it from the Mystery Box or the vault, but crafting it is free and guaranteed.

Free Power-Ups & Every Perk

Eight power-ups are hidden around town, each in a fixed spot, each claimable once per game by shooting the little floating icon. Save them for when they hurt most — don't pop the Max Ammo with full mags. A scoped weapon or C4 makes the awkward angles easy.

One-Run Order — Miss Nothing

A single efficient route that threads everything together, roughly by round. Leave the last zombie of each round alive while you run errands — that's the whole trick to Zombies routing.

Rounds 1–4 · Spawn & East Side

  1. On the roof: shoot the Max Ammo icon on the bridge only if you need it — otherwise save it. Drop down.
  2. Shoot bowling shoes #1 on the Pump & Pay counter. Melee the vending machine (round 1 of many).
  3. Open east to Riverside. In Olly's Comics: grab “Quantum Mechanical Failure,” search the comic boxes for “Final Issue,” note the vault code under the counter, prone at Quick Revive for 100.
  4. Shoot shoes #2 in Frank's Hardware across the street, and the 3 tapes in the VHS store window for “Fine Man.”
  5. Shoot the Bonus Points icon on the AC unit behind Olly's. Use both telescopes for the zombie rave while the area is quiet.

Rounds 5–8 · Open the Town, Start the Jet Gun

  1. Open Washington Ave: take the Water Valve at Lily's Flowerpot, grab “Conspiracy Theorist” by Speed Cola, shoot the Max Armor icon through the blue-house window and the Double Points icon on the water tower.
  2. Open the west route too: Liberty Lanes — install the valve, build pressure, take the Water Pressure Gauge. Shoot the pool balls (+100), melee the snack-bar bucket for vault note #3, shoot the Full Power icon behind the bus outside.
  3. Headset #1 in Motor Lodge Alley, headset #2 under the Washington Ave bench. Open Room 202 and grab “The Scientist and The Moonshiner.”
  4. First Mangler: shoot its arm off, take the Mangler Cannon to Radio House for the Electrical Wires — then blow up the Mr. Peeks car if he's spawned (document chance).

Rounds 9–13 · Church, Jet Gun, Vault

  1. Cemetery run: kill the Groundskeeper for the Toolshed Key → Handbrake. Build the Jet Gun in Room 202.
  2. Same trip: Deadshot cans + Insta-Kill skull at the back fence, shoes #4 on the tree, “The House Josiah Built” by the armor buy, shoes #3 in the Hill Street white house on the way down.
  3. Church: SDG Generator (quest starts), headset #3, “Bear Witness,” Napalm Burst the unlit candles, explosive the spire head for raining loot. Vacuum the church LTG part.
  4. Bank: read vault note #1, enter the code, loot boxes with your Loot Keys (“Money Talks” + “Politics, Politics” chances), shoot the Nuke icon on the chandelier. Shoes #5 on the funeral-home coffin across from the lanes — that teleports you into bowling. Go for 300.
  5. Vacuum the Olly's Comics + Buzz's Cuts LTG parts, build the LTG on the Alamo, and knock out all 9 Aetherella figures while you have the Jet Gun out — then grab “Limited Run” back in Olly's.

Rounds 14+ · Finish the Quest, Sweep the Rest

  1. Charge canister #1 (portal defense → purple-glowing HVT → trap). Return it, do the 3 Strauss projectors, grab “One Lit Match” in Groundskeeper's Yard while you're there.
  2. Charge canister #2 at the other portal. Somewhere in here: Blood Pool (3 Semtex from the bank roof), Grave Digger (Chopper Gunner the barrel → shovel → dig every grave), vending machine every round, Peeks car every ~5 rounds.
  3. Claim the Fire Sale by PhD Flopper once the other 7 power-ups are shot.
  4. Gear up fully, then hit the SDG Generator for the final encounter. After the cutscene: keep playing with your bonus perks, or exfil at Pump & Pay.
  5. Round 25+: the Wunderfizz appears on the bank roof — fill out any perks you're missing, and replay bowling via the toilet shoes if you still need the 300-point calling card.
Solo survival basics: Jugger-Nog first, then Speed Cola and Stamin-Up. Keep a Decoy or Monkey Bomb for the water-pressure and portal-defense steps. Train zombies in the Cemetery or the church forecourt — the two widest loops on the map. Never fire an Aether trap until the HVT is glowing purple and standing in it.
