01

The New Rules

Cold War rebuilt Zombies from the ground up. Wall-buys and the Mystery Box still exist, but almost everything around them is new. Master these six systems and every map gets easier.

Loadouts

Bring Your Own Gun

You start with a Gunsmith weapon from your own loadout — attachments included. It starts at Common (grey) rarity and you upgrade its damage by raising rarity, not just Pack-a-Punching.

  • Start with a strong AR, LMG or shotgun blueprint
  • Your knife is a real melee option early
  • Field Upgrade + lethal/tactical come with you
Health & Armour

150 Base Health + Armour

Base health is 150. Armour comes in 1, 2 and 3 plates (bought at the Armour Station or crafted) and soaks damage before health does. Juggernog still matters — it raises health to 250.

  • Armour breaks plate by plate — re-plate often
  • Self-Revive Kits can be crafted or bought
  • Health regen kicks in after a few seconds clear
Salvage & Crafting

Two Kinds of Salvage

Zombies drop Salvage (green) and rarer High-Grade Salvage (blue). Spend it at the Crafting Table on armour, Self-Revives, equipment, support streaks and ammo mods.

  • Don’t hoard it — a Self-Revive beats 500 saved salvage
  • Salvage is per-player, not shared
Weapon Rarity

Grey → Gold, 5 Steps

Common → Uncommon (green) → Rare (blue) → Epic (purple) → Legendary (orange/gold). Each step is a big damage jump. Raise it at the Arsenal with Salvage, or find higher-rarity guns in the Box and as drops.

  • Rarity and Pack-a-Punch stack — you need both for high rounds
Pack-a-Punch

3 Tiers, Essence Only

Tier I: 5,000 • Tier II: 15,000 • Tier III: 30,000 Essence. No parts to build on most maps — turn on power, reach the machine, pay. Tier III roughly triples your damage output with rarity stacked.

  • Re-Packing changes your Ammo Mod
  • Apply an Ammo Mod separately at the machine
Aetherium & Tiers

Skills Go to Tier V

Every perk, Field Upgrade and Ammo Mod has 5 skill tiers unlocked with Aetherium Crystals earned by playing (and refined/perfected crystals at higher milestones). Tier V perks are game-changers — prioritise Juggernog, your Field Upgrade, and Deadshot first.

  • Crystals are account-wide — progress carries across maps
Ammo Mods — pick one per gun

Napalm Burst (fire DoT, best vs Megatons) • Dead Wire (electric stun chains) • Cryofreeze (slows everything — safest for high rounds) • Shatter Blast (explosive burst, great on armour) • Brain Rot (turns a zombie to fight for you — elite turned zombies clear trains). At Tier V most mods also shred special enemies. Cryofreeze + Brain Rot is the classic two-gun combo.

Perks — All 10

No perk limit. Seriously — you can buy every perk on the map. Prices rise with each one you own: 2,500 / 3,000 / 3,500 / 4,000, then +500 each after. Wunderfizz gives a random unowned perk and appears once you own 4+ (or from Round 10+ on some maps). You only lose perks one at a time when downed, in reverse buy order — and Tombstone can save the lot.

Standard buy order (solo)

Juggernog → Quick Revive → Deadshot → Stamin-Up → Speed Cola → then Wunderfizz the rest. In co-op, drop Quick Revive to 4th and take Deadshot 2nd.

03

The Maps

Four round-based maps tell one connected Dark Aether story — Requiem vs Omega Group, Samantha Maxis, and the Forsaken himself. Play them in order if you care about the story; jump to Forsaken if you just want the best training map.

Launch Map • Poland

Die Maschine

The Nacht der Untoten facility, cracked open into the Dark Aether.

Wonder Weapon: D.I.E. ShockwaveDifficulty: Easiest questBest for: Learning

A two-layer map: the classic Nacht layout on the surface, a Particle Accelerator facility underneath, and a Dark Aether version of the whole map you phase in and out of to build Pack-a-Punch. Wide yard for training, tight facility corners for camping. The friendliest first main quest in the game.

Season 1 • Vietnam

Firebase Z

Omega’s jungle firebase built around a dimensional portal — and a trapped Samantha.

Wonder Weapon: RAI K-84New enemy: MimicBest for: Camping

Two zones linked by a teleporter: the spawn Village and the Firebase itself. Turn on three Aether Reactors instead of one power switch. Defence rounds at the reactors teach you to hold ground. The helipad / mission control area is one of the best camp spots in Cold War.

Season 4 • Berlin

Mauer der Toten

“Wall of the Dead” — rooftops and U-Bahn tunnels in divided Berlin.

Wonder Weapon: CRBR-SAlly: Klaus (buildable robot)Best for: Vertical play

The most vertical map in the game: streets, rooftops with ziplines, a sewer and a subway. You build Klaus, a friendly robot who revives and fights for you, and fight the Panzer Soldat’s successor — plus a train that will happily run you over. Hardest round-based quest of the four.

Season 6 • Final Map • Ukraine

Forsaken

An Anytown, USA test site hiding the Forsaken’s containment chamber.

Wonder Weapon: ChrysalaxBoss: The Forsaken / AbominationBest for: High rounds

The finale. A fake American main street over a bunker, a proper final boss fight, and the Chrysalax — an axe/gun hybrid forged from Dark Aether crystals. The rooftop and video-store areas are elite training ground. Finish this quest and you’ve finished the Cold War story.

Also in the game

Dead Ops Arcade 3 — a full top-down twin-stick arcade mode with its own maps, bosses and 60+ levels. Onslaught — fast 2-player survival on multiplayer maps against a moving Orb (launched as a PlayStation exclusive, later opened to all). Outbreak gets its own full section below — it’s half the game.

04

Main Quest Walkthroughs

Every Cold War main quest follows the same rhythm: power → Pack-a-Punch → Wonder Weapon → collection steps → boss or finale. Steps below are the proven solo-friendly routes. Do quests before Round 15 where you can — scaling makes late steps miserable.

Die Maschine — “Seal the Deal”

Easiest • 45–60 min
  1. Power & Pack-a-Punch.

    Open to the Particle Accelerator, turn on power, then enter the Dark Aether portal. In the Aether, go to the Medical Bay / Crash Site spots to pick up the missing machine part, return, and build Pack-a-Punch at the accelerator.

  2. Get the D.I.E. Shockwave.

    Kill a Megaton — it splits into two Megaton halves (Bomber + Blaster). Kill both, take the keycard, open the Weapons Lab safe for the D.I.E. Remote, then suck up energy at the glowing fungi / box in the Yard to charge and claim the D.I.E.

  3. Build all 4 elemental D.I.E. upgrades (quest needs them).

    Electrobolt: in Dark Aether, shoot crystals in Pond / Penthouse areas. Gas / Nova 5: suck the canister under the plane at Crash Site, cleanse it at the box. Cryo / Icewave: shoot the frozen box on the truck at Penthouse. Thermophase: suck up the fuse at Crash Site in the Aether and place it in the Tank room box.

    Tip: You only strictly need the variants for the containment steps — but having Cryo for the finale defence is worth the detour.
  4. Talk to the anomaly & collect the photos.

    Interact with the anomaly in the Medical Bay (Dark Aether), speak to the three spectral figures around the map, and collect the items they point you to.

  5. Charge the containment unit.

    Use each D.I.E. variant on the matching canister at the Particle Accelerator to fill the containment unit — defend between charges.

  6. Finale — protect the unit & the decontamination.

    Survive the locked-in accelerator defence waves (Megatons + Plaguehounds). Keep Ring of Fire for the Megaton spawns. Survive to the end for the cutscene and Exfil prompt.

Firebase Z — Rescue Samantha

Medium • 60–75 min
  1. Power via 3 Aether Reactors.

    Teleport from the Village to the Firebase, then activate all three reactors (Military Command, Mission Control, Village side). Each costs Essence and triggers a defence — let zombies come to you, don’t chase.

  2. Free Samantha — talk & data.

    Speak to Ravenov in Mission Control and Dr. Peck through the window. Collect the data / serum steps: get the compound from the locker (key from a Mimic kill in the Village area — Mimics hide as loot items, listen for the whisper).

  3. Build the RAI K-84.

    Get the blueprint from the Weapons Lab trial / dartboard code (shoot the dartboard numbers in order: the code shows on the board), collect the parts — barrel from the Mangler drop, battery charged at the reactor, and assemble at the Weapons Lab bench. Or pull it from the Box / Trials.

  4. Uranium & the device.

    Collect the two uranium rocks (from special enemies at the marked spots), process them at the workbenches, and deliver the device components to Ravenov.

  5. Charge the Aethermeter & capture essences.

    Place canisters at the marked Aether spots and trap special zombie essences by killing the right enemy type inside the trap radius.

  6. Finale — defend the portal & fight Orda.

    Activate the portal in Mission Control, survive the lockdown, then face Orda — a giant Elder God — in the Village. Shoot the glowing weak points, use the RAI K-84’s alternate fire to slow adds, and keep moving: Orda’s slam covers a huge area. Ring of Fire on the weak-point phases ends it fast.

Mauer der Toten — Free Klaus, Stop Valentina

Hardest • 75–100 min
  1. Power & Pack-a-Punch.

    Open through the streets to the Power Room in the sewer/subway area, flip the switch, kill the Tempests that spawn, then build Pack-a-Punch with the part in the Dark Aether-adjacent step by the machine.

  2. Build Klaus.

    Collect: battery from a Panzer Soldat kill, hands from the Hotel room (safe code from the brick wall combination in the alley), and the microwave dish from the Garment Factory pile. Assemble Klaus at the Safe House. Upgrade him at the upgrade station with zombie kills nearby — you’ll need upgraded Klaus later.

    Tip: Klaus revives you and holds a train like a champ. Keep him upgraded all game, not just for the quest.
  3. Get the CRBR-S.

    From the Box, Trials, or by having Klaus kill zombies near the Safe House safe — open the safe for the base CRBR-S. Its mod kits (Blaze, Diffuser, Swarm) drop from enemies and change its fire mode.

  4. Activate the three antennas / satellite step.

    Use the CRBR-S and Klaus to power the marked terminals around the rooftops and streets in order, collecting the essence canisters from special kills at each.

  5. Uranium enrichment.

    Collect uranium from the zipline / rooftop spots (use the ziplines with the canister — don’t fall), process it at the Safe House device, and build the warhead components.

  6. Finale — Valentina boss fight.

    Enter the portal arena and fight Valentina in phases: shoot her shield down, damage phase, add-clear phase with Disciples and Tormentors. Stay on the move on the rooftops of the arena, save Ring of Fire for damage phases, and finish with the H.A.R.P./warhead sequence for the ending.

Forsaken — Confront The Forsaken

Hard • Final Boss • 75–90 min
  1. Power, teleporter & Pack-a-Punch.

    Work through Anytown’s Main Street into the Bunker, activate the teleporter and fuel it (kill the Abomination / collect fuel), then reach the Observation Tower area to unlock Pack-a-Punch.

  2. Forge the Chrysalax.

    Collect a Catalyzed Crystal Shard (Abomination kill), a Dark Aether crystal heart, and the other forge parts marked around the map; temper them at the Forge in the Bunker. The Chrysalax swaps between a battle axe (melee, huge damage) and a rapid-fire gun form.

  3. Build the Aetherium Neutralizer.

    Gather the housing, fuel tank and monitor parts (arcade / video store / bunker spots — the arcade cabinet step needs a zombie fed to the machine), and assemble the device.

  4. Escort & charge the Neutralizer.

    Push the device through the marked zones, defending it while it charges on crystals. Abominations spawn on this step — Chrysalax axe form deletes them.

  5. Open the containment door.

    Deliver the Neutralizer, watch the story beat, and prepare fully before triggering the finale: Tier III Pack, Legendary rarity, full perks, Self-Revive, Ring of Fire charged.

  6. Boss — The Forsaken.

    A true multi-phase boss: destroy the glowing crystal weak points on his body while Samantha (in the mech / support role) charges her attack. Dodge the Aether orbs, clear Disciple adds between phases, and burn each weak point inside Ring of Fire. Three phases and the Cold War saga ends.

05

Outbreak — The Big Mode

Outbreak drops you into huge open regions (the Fireteam maps: Alpine, Ruka, Golova, Sanatorium, Duga, Zoo, Collateral, Armada) with one main objective per region, then a warp to the next region at higher difficulty. It’s the best mode for levelling guns, earning crystals, and relaxed co-op — and it has two full main quests of its own.

Objectives — know them cold

The 6 Objective Types

  • Defend: hold the Aetherium canister machine for waves
  • Escort: protect the rover as it drives to the portal
  • Retrieve: carry Aetherium canisters to the rockets (slows you down — drop & fight)
  • Eliminate: kill the marked Elite (HVT) — Mangler, Mimic, Krasny Soldat
  • Holdout: survive locked inside a building until extraction
  • Secure: stay in the marked zone and fill the meter

Between objectives: loot everything. Golden chests, Trials, world events (Fury Crystal, Dragon / Demented Echo, fishing at ponds) and the Mystery Box all spawn per region. Always leave a region fully looted — you can’t go back.

Side content that pays

Don’t Skip These

  • Trials machine: spend Essence for challenges → Legendary rewards incl. free perks & Pack levels
  • Red / Gold rifts & orbs: shoot the floating orb, follow it — free loot & perks
  • World events: the dragon circle event drops high-rarity weapons early
  • Vehicles: sedans & trucks run over whole hordes — best “weapon” in Regions 1–2
  • Fishing: genuinely drops salvage, equipment and occasionally great loot

Warp rule: difficulty, enemy health and Elite counts scale hard from Region 4+. Bank a Tier II Pack and Rare+ weapon before warping past Region 3.

Outbreak Main Quest 1 — “Operation Excision” (Legion)

Medium • Do in Regions 1–3
  1. Find the Omega recon & red rift.

    Progress regions until the quest beacon appears; interact with the Omega documents / radio to start tracking the rift signal.

  2. Collect the recon intel & beacons.

    Place and defend the micro-storm beacons at the marked spots in the region; kill the waves that attack each beacon.

  3. Enter the red rift → Missile Silo.

    Warp takes you to the Silo (under Sanatorium). Work down the silo levels, activating consoles in order while fighting in tight halls — shotguns shine here.

  4. Boss — Legion.

    A giant floating Tempest orb. Shoot the glowing orbs / energy balls it launches to charge your own damage window, then unload on Legion’s core when it opens. Clear Tempest adds first every time they spawn — they’re the real killer in this fight.

Outbreak Main Quest 2 — Entrapment / The Final Revelation

Hard • Unlocks after Quest 1
  1. Start in a later region.

    The quest activates from the Omega helicopter / Ravenov radio step once Excision is done. Follow the signal to the marked region (often Ruka / Sanatorium side areas).

  2. Collect the Maxis intel & escort steps.

    Complete the marked defend/escort sequence protecting the device while Samantha’s signal is traced.

  3. The trap & the reveal.

    Survive the ambush waves at the extraction site — this is the hardest add-wave in Outbreak. Save support streaks (Chopper Gunner / Death Machine from crafting) for this.

  4. Exfil under fire.

    Reach the Exfil chopper with the whole team alive. Completing this sets up the Forsaken finale story — and unlocks the related Dark Ops / calling-card rewards.

Exfil & Progression Tips

New in Cold War

How Exfil Works

From Round 10 onward, every 5 rounds the Exfil radio (marked on your HUD) offers an escape. Activate it, reach the Exfil site, and clear every zombie in the zone before the timer ends — then the chopper leaves with you on it.

  • Successful Exfil pays bonus Essence, XP and Raw Aetherium Crystals
  • The Exfil wave spawns Elites — save your Field Upgrade and support for it
  • Failing the timer doesn’t end the game — you just keep playing to the next window
  • Solo players: Aether Shroud to reach the site, Ring of Fire to clear it
Get strong faster

Progression Priority

  • Spend your first crystals on: Juggernog tiers → your Field Upgrade → Deadshot → Speed Cola
  • Level guns in Outbreak — big maps, easy kills, weapon XP flows
  • Do the daily & seasonal Zombies challenges — they pay crystals, not just XP
  • Finish each map’s quest once on any difficulty — Dark Ops and rewards are account-wide
  • Play Forsaken last in story order; its boss assumes you know every system above
The 5 mistakes that end Cold War runs

1) Skipping armour re-plates because “health is full” — armour IS your health.  2) Pack-a-Punching before raising rarity (or the reverse) — you need both.  3) Opening every door early and shrinking your training space.  4) Picking up loot without checking for a Mimic.  5) Starting a main-quest finale without a Self-Revive, full perks and a charged Field Upgrade.
