2 Story & The Two Leads

A Bonnie-and-Clyde story: two partners who have always known the deck is stacked against them, pulled into a state-wide conspiracy after an easy score goes wrong.

Playable Lead

Lucia Caminos

Fresh out of the Leonida Penitentiary at the start of the game. Grew up in Liberty City; her father taught her to fight as a kid, and fighting for her family is what landed her inside. She wants the peaceful life her mother always dreamed of — and sees Jason, and smarter choices, as the way to get it. Lucia is the first playable female lead in a numbered GTA story.

Playable Lead

Jason Duval

Grew up around grifters and trouble, tried to straighten out with a stint in the Army, then landed in the Leonida Keys working for local drug runners. He runs jobs and shakedowns for veteran smuggler Brian Heder, and lives in one of Brian's properties in exchange for the work. He wants an easier life — the game has other plans.

Playing as both

Unlike GTA V's separate solo missions, Jason and Lucia work as a team and take on missions together. Launch coverage confirms you can switch between them at any time — even mid-drive. Lean into that: when a job splits the pair, the switch is the mechanic, not a cutscene trick.

3 The Cast

Rockstar published eight official character profiles — Jason and Lucia (above) plus these six. Voice cast was only fully credited at launch — don't trust older "leaked cast" lists.

Leonida Keys

Brian Heder

Veteran drug runner from the golden age of Keys smuggling. Runs Brian's Boat Works & Marina, keeps his hands off direct client work, and is Jason's boss and landlord. On his third wife, Lori.

Leonida Keys

Cal Hampton

Jason's friend and fellow Brian associate. Paranoid, conspiratorial, happiest at home with a few beers, snooping on Coast Guard comms and watching the birds a little too closely.

Vice City

Boobie Ike

Street hustler turned legitimate empire: real estate, the Jack of Hearts strip club, and a recording studio. All smiles until it's business. His passion project is the Only Raw Records label.

Vice City

Dre'Quan Priest

More hustler than gangster — dealt to survive, but music was always the goal. Boobie's partner at Only Raw Records; he signed Real Dimez and wants out of booking strip-club acts and into the big Vice City scene.

Vice City

Real Dimez — Bae-Luxe & Roxy

Rap duo and school friends with one viral hit behind them, signed to Only Raw Records. The act Dre'Quan is betting the label on.

Statewide

Raul Bautista

Seasoned bank robber — confidence, charm and cunning, always scouting fresh talent for bigger scores. Experienced, but reckless enough that it may catch up with him.

4 Leonida — The Map

Rockstar's take on Florida: one big city, five more regions, five counties. Rockstar has not published an official map size — anyone quoting exact square mileage is guessing.

Vice City

"Everything in Excess"
Vice-Dale County

The neon metropolis — Miami-inspired beaches, pastel hotels, Ocean Beach, Washington Beach, Watson Bay, the cruise port and VCIA airport. First full return since Vice City Stories (2006). Boobie and Dre'Quan's turf.

Leonida Keys

"Gateway to Paradise"
Island chain · South

Tropical islands linked by highway bridges, running from Key Lento down to Mile Zero. Jason's home turf — marinas, boat yards, and Brian's smuggling operation.

Grassrivers

"Welcome to the Wetlands"
Mariana County

Everglades-style wetlands south-west of Vice City: mangroves, airboats, alligators, and isolated, dangerous backcountry. Pack an off-roader or a boat, not a supercar.

Port Gellhorn

"Live Hard"
Kelly County · West coast

Leonida's forgotten coast — a faded Gulf resort town of cheap motels, strip clubs and empty strip malls. The seedy opposite of Vice City's shine.

Ambrosia

"Keeping Leonida Sweet"
State centre

The agricultural and industrial heartland around Lake Leonida, dominated by the big sugar refinery. Working towns, farmland and industry — where Leonida's money is actually made.

Mount Kalaga National Park

The wild north
Lummox County

Forested hills on the northern border: hunting, fishing, kayaking and off-road trails, with backwoods communities hidden in the trees. The place to disappear.

5 Your First Hours — Checklist

Starter advice for launch week. It teaches the game's systems in order — don't rush past it to free-roam and wonder why nothing's unlocked.

  • Finish the opening story beats first. Lucia's release and the reunion with Jason set up the duo-switching, safehouses and your first contacts.
  • Learn the switch. Practise swapping between Jason and Lucia in low-stakes moments — later jobs assume you can do it under pressure.
  • Bank your cash, don't carry it all. A series staple habit: dying and arrests hurt less when your money is safe. Verify in-game
  • Claim the pre-order Stainer and the Ocean Beach garage if you pre-ordered — free wheels and storage from hour one.
  • Drive the Keys causeway once, slowly. Best intro to the map's scale, and it unlocks your mental map of Jason's home region.
  • Don't blow early money on clothes and rims. Weapons, armour and a reliable getaway car pay off first. Style comes after the first big score.
  • Save before every score. Heist-style jobs branch — a clean save lets you replay a botched approach instead of living with it.

6 Systems — What We Know

Confirmed

Duo gameplay

Two playable leads who mission together, with free switching at any time, including while driving. Relationship-driven story in the Bonnie-and-Clyde mould.

Confirmed

A living, social world

Trailers show a Leonida obsessed with social media — livestreams, viral clips and phones everywhere. Expect the world to react to, and record, what you do.

Confirmed

Water is half the map

Keys, Grassrivers, lakes and coast mean boats and airboats are core transport, not novelties. Learn the marinas early.

Unconfirmed

Wanted system, stats & properties

Exact wanted-level behaviour, character stats, buyable businesses and property lists are still being documented by players. This section gets rewritten once verified — we won't guess numbers here.

Survival habits that always pay in GTA

  • Keep armour topped up before story jobs; buy snacks for quick health.
  • Break line of sight to lose heat — alleys, water and the Grassrivers mangroves beat straight-line speed.
  • Keep one fast, forgettable car for scores. Exotic cars get remembered — and so do you.
  • In the swamps and Kalaga backcountry, watch for wildlife. The gators are not decoration.

8 FAQ

Is GTA 6 on PC, PS4 or Xbox One?

No. Launch is PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and Rockstar has not announced a PC date. Anyone selling a "PC version" right now is scamming.

Is GTA Online in GTA 6 at launch?

No — a new Online mode is not available at launch and has no announced date. GTA V Online is still the online game for now.

Can I play as just Jason or just Lucia?

The story is built around the pair working together, with free switching between them — including mid-drive. It's a duo game by design.

Is this the same Vice City as the 2002 game?

Same city, modern day. This is a present-day reimagining — new story, new cast, vastly bigger state around it — not a remake of the 2002 game.

How big is the map?

Unconfirmed Rockstar hasn't published an official size. It's a full state — city, keys, swamps, farmland and a national park — and clearly the largest GTA map to date, but exact figures circulating online are estimates.

Which edition should I buy?

Standard ($79.99) for most players. Ultimate ($99.99) only if you specifically want the premium vehicles, weapons and apparel bonuses.
