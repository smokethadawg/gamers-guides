Start Here — What's New in BO2

BO2 keeps the classic points economy from BO1 and layers four big systems on top. Everything below applies unless a map card says otherwise.

Buildables & workbenches

  • Parts are carried one at a time. Pick up a part, take it to a workbench, and build — shields, turrets, traps, and wonder weapons all work this way.
  • The Zombie Shield (buildable on most maps) blocks hits from behind and can be re-bought off the bench when it breaks. Build it early, every game.
  • Buildables break and wear out. Turbines die, Trample Steams get stomped, the Jet Gun overheats. Plan around replacing them.
  • Nav Cards & buildable tables: each story map hides a Nav Card from the previous map — carry them forward to unlock the shared Easter egg steps.

Bank, locker & Double Tap II

  • The Bank (TranZit, Die Rise & Buried — all three share one account): deposit points in one game, withdraw them in the next — 1,000 points at a time, with a small fee on withdrawal. Banked points are how you fund fast starts.
  • The weapon locker / fridge (same three maps, also shared): store a gun at the end of a game and pull it out on any of the three maps next game. Store something Pack-a-Punched.
  • Double Tap II replaces the old Double Tap: every trigger pull now fires two bullets for the price of one, plus the fire-rate boost. It is a top-three perk in BO2, not a situational one.
  • Persistent upgrades (TranZit / Green Run): hidden stat boosts earned by play — headshot damage, faster revives, stronger boards — shown by a green flash when earned. They can be lost if you play badly.

The Maps

In release order — the five story maps first, then the survival maps. Use the filters above to jump around.

TRZLaunch

TranZit

Green Run, Washington · The bus ride across the fog

Wonder Weapon: Jet GunBoss: The AvogadroDifficulty: Hard

Power, the bus & Pack-a-Punch

The bus (driven by T.E.D.D.) loops between Bus Depot, Diner, Farm, Power Station, and Town — ride it or run the fog at your own risk. Power is at the Power Station: build the switch at the workbench (circuit board, lever, and a zombie arm from the shed area), then flip it. Pack-a-Punch is in the bank vault in Town — blow the vault doors open with explosives (grenades work) after power is on. That same vault holds the Bank (deposit/withdrawal keys on the wall), and the weapon locker is the fridge inside the Farm house — both systems debut on this map and are shared with Die Rise and Buried. Build the Turbine at the Bus Depot bench (mannequin, fan blade, tail fin) — it powers doors, traps, and perk machines without the main power, and it is the most useful buildable in BO2.

Survival notes

  • Denizens leap on your head in the fog — knife them off (5 knives) or stay on the bus and on lit roads.
  • Turning the power on wakes the Avogadro, an electric man who spawns in the cornfield and zaps you at range. Knife him to drive him off — shooting barely slows him. Many players simply never turn the power on and run Turbines instead.
  • The Jet Gun (Thrustodyne AER4) is built from parts in the tunnel, the diner area, the power station, and the cornfield cabin (Nacht der Untoten easter egg building). It sucks in whole trains and explodes them — but it overheats and breaks, so fire in bursts.
  • Train at the Diner parking lot or the Town street loop. The Farm barn is a solid early holdout.

Main Easter egg — Tower of Babble (pick a side: Maxis or Richtofen)

  1. Build the Turbine, turn the power ON at the Power Station, and build the Nav Card table under the transmission tower in the cornfield.
  2. Maxis path: turn the power back OFF. Build the Jet Gun and use it under the tower in the cornfield until it overheats and Maxis confirms the charge. Then place two Turbines at two street-lamp posts and throw an EMP grenade at a lit lamp post to complete the circuit.
  3. Richtofen path: leave the power ON. Trap the Avogadro under the tower (lure him there on a power-surge round), place Turbines under the tower and at lamp posts, and kill zombies beneath the tower to feed it souls while the Avogadro is held there.
  4. Either path ends with the tower lit in your side's color — blue for Maxis, orange for Richtofen. Your choice carries into Die Rise and Buried, so a regular crew should agree on one side and stick with it across all three maps.

Song: “Carrion” by Kevin Sherwood — activate the three teddy bears (Bus Depot, Farm house, Town bar area).

DIEDLC 1 · Revolution

Die Rise

Crumbling Shanghai skyscrapers · Watch your step

Wonder Weapon: SliquifierSpecial: Minions (Jumping Jacks)Difficulty: Hard

Power, elevators & Pack-a-Punch

You start on a rooftop and drop into the building — power is reached by riding the elevator shafts down through the ruined tower (the key spawns in the shaft areas; elevators move on their own, so time your rides). Perks ride elevators too: machines sit in shafts and arrive on different floors each visit. Pack-a-Punch is on the roof of the second building, reached across the Trample Steam gap. The Bank here is a pair of shower stalls just outside the power room, and the weapon locker is the blue fridge next to them — both are the same shared systems from TranZit, and they carry into Buried too.

Survival notes

  • Falling is the real boss. The map is holes, shafts, and ledges. Learn the safe drops; Who's Who exists on this map for a reason.
  • The Sliquifier is built at the bench in the sweatshop area (parts around the starting building and the power floor). It coats the floor in slippery goo and melts groups — upgraded, it carries high rounds almost alone.
  • The Trample Steam (buildable) flings zombies — and you. Set one facing the roof gap to cross safely, and on ledges as a trap.
  • Minion rounds (leaping crawlers) every few rounds — the last one drops a Max Ammo or a free perk. Train on the Buddha room floor once you reach it: the widest flat loop on the map.

Main Easter egg — High Maintenance (continues your TranZit side)

  1. Turn on power and get all four players standing on the four golden symbols in the elevator shafts at the same time until they lock in.
  2. Build the Sliquifier and coat the floor symbols in the starting building; then place Trample Steams on the dragon symbols so flung zombies land on the marked pads and charge them.
  3. Shoot the orbs free from the two rooftop dragons' mouths, then use the Galvaknuckles to punch the four tower pillars in the order shown by the Mahjong tiles hidden around the map (the correct order matches the tile set you found).
  4. Finish the final charge in the Buddha room area as your side's voice directs. Reward: a free perk slot reward / all-perks drop depending on path, plus the story beat that sets up Buried. As with TranZit, Maxis and Richtofen paths have different steps mid-way — follow the voice you chose.

Song: “We All Fall Down” by Kevin Sherwood — activate the three teddy bears.

MOBDLC 2 · Uprising

Mob of the Dead

Alcatraz Island prison, 1930s · The fan-favorite

Wonder Weapons: Blundergat + Hell's RetrieverBoss: Brutus the WardenDifficulty: Medium–Hard

Afterlife, power & Pack-a-Punch

There is no power switch. Instead, dying (or using a life token) sends you into Afterlife mode: as a ghost you shock generators to power doors, perk machines, and the plane parts. You get one Afterlife per round plus earned extras — spend them wisely. Pack-a-Punch is on the Golden Gate Bridge, reached by building the plane on the roof (five parts, each collected in Afterlife: fuel, rigging, engine, uniforms, controls) and flying it across. The plane needs refueling for later trips.

Brutus & the gear

  • Brutus storms in every few rounds, locks down perk machines and the box, and smashes buildables. Headshots stagger him; killing him drops a power-up. Don't let him lock Juggernog mid-fight.
  • Feed zombies to the three glowing Cerberus heads (infirmary, cafeteria, docks) to unlock the Hell's Retriever tomahawk in the lava pit — it one-kills into the mid-teens and picks up drops from range.
  • The Blundergat is earned by finding five skulls with the Retriever (on posts and rooftops around the prison), then collecting it from the Warden's office. Build the Acid Gat kit at a bench to convert it into an acid-spewing monster; the upgraded Magma Gat is one of BO2's best guns.
  • The Golden Spork — the strongest melee in the game — is hidden behind a poster in the cafeteria area and a spoon-retrieval sequence in the infirmary. Worth it: it one-hit kills to round 30+.

Main Easter egg — Pop Goes the Weasel

  1. Collect all five plane parts using Afterlife shocks, build the plane, and fly to the Golden Gate Bridge. Use the electric chairs to return.
  2. Refuel and fly three times total, completing the Retriever and Blundergat side quests between trips.
  3. Enter Afterlife on the bridge and read the prisoner numbers shown at the cell block number pad (by the docks) — enter the three codes into the keypad in the cell block while a teammate guards you.
  4. Final step: on the bridge, the team fights a wave of Brutus spawns. The player playing Weasel (Arlington) must stay in Afterlife at the end to break the cycle — or revive and let the cycle continue. Breaking the cycle is the true ending; continuing it lets the game play on.

Song: “Where Are We Going” by Kevin Sherwood (feat. Malukah) — activate the three teddy bears / the 935 bottles. Johnny Cash's “Rusty Cage” plays in the opening area radio.

BURDLC 3 · Vengeance

Buried

Buried Old West town, Resolution 1295 · Leroy carries

Wonder Weapons: Paralyzer + Time BombHelper: Leroy (the Giant)Difficulty: Medium

Power, Leroy & Pack-a-Punch

You drop through the processing tunnels into the town. There is no power switch for the map itself — machines run on their own — but everything important runs through Leroy, the giant locked in the jail. Free him with the key, then manage him: give him booze (from the saloon) and he'll smash the debris blocking buildings; give him candy (from the candy store) and he'll attack zombies, carry buildables to benches, or hold a crawler for you. Pack-a-Punch sits in the haunted mansion's maze-side area — Leroy smashing the fountain debris opens the fast route. The Bank and weapon locker return here (the locker is in the General Store), shared with TranZit and Die Rise.

Survival notes

  • The Paralyzer (box wonder weapon) slows zombies to a crawl in a beam and never runs out of ammo — it overheats instead. It is the best crowd-control gun in BO2 and lets you fly briefly by aiming down.
  • The Time Bomb (buildable) records the round state — detonate it to rewind everyone to that moment, guns, points, and all. Set one before every risky step.
  • Chalk outlines: grab chalk pieces around town and draw wall-buy outlines where you want them — you choose your own wall weapons, and drawing all of them pays a points bonus.
  • The Subsurface Resonator + Turbine combo at the town entrance mulches whole trains hands-free — the classic Buried high-round setup. Train in the saloon street or by the church.
  • The mansion ghosts drain 100 points per touch — sprint through, don't fight them.

Main Easter egg — Mined Games (the Victis finale)

  1. Free Leroy, build the Nav Card table in the courthouse, and build the Time Bomb. Have Leroy smash the fountain and the mansion-route debris.
  2. Follow the wisp: charge it at the electric switch in the tunnels, then use Vulture Aid's glow to track it through the hedge maze to the next switch — repeat as it relocates.
  3. Place the Time Bomb by the fountain, go back, and shoot the four glowing orbs (mansion, jail, church, and saloon areas) with the Paralyzer in the past state — then return and hit the switch in the courthouse.
  4. Final step: all four players take the shooting-gallery challenge at the saloon targets — every target must be hit in the sequence. Reward: all perks on the map, permanently for the game, and the Victis story ends on your chosen side (Maxis or Richtofen, decided by your TranZit/Die Rise choices).

Song: “Always Running” by Kevin Sherwood (feat. Malukah) — activate the three teddy bears.

ORIDLC 4 · Apocalypse

Origins

WWI France, 1918 · Giant robots & the four staffs

Wonder Weapons: The 4 Elemental StaffsBoss: Panzer SoldatDifficulty: Very Hard

Generators, robots & Pack-a-Punch

No single power switch: six generators power their local areas, and zombies will attack active generators to shut them down — defend them or re-capture them. Capture all six to unlock the Pack-a-Punch area under the Excavation Site. Three giant robots (Odin, Thor, Freya) stomp across the map on a cycle — their feet kill you, but stepping in the glowing foot at the right moment rides you inside to a staff part room. Der Wunderfizz sells a random perk for 1,500 — the only place PHD Flopper appears in BO2. Dig spots with the shovel (golden shovel after enough digs) for parts, guns, and the occasional zombie ambush.

Panzer Soldat & the staffs

  • The Panzer Soldat drops in from round 8 onward — a flamethrower armored boss whose claw drags you in. Aim for the glowing fuel tank and head; killing him drops staff parts and power-ups. He returns every few rounds, sometimes two at once.
  • Each staff is built from three parts plus an elemental crystal fetched from the Crazy Place (opened by powering the four ritual pedestals): Fire (red), Ice (blue), Lightning (purple), Wind (yellow).
  • Every staff upgrades through its own puzzle — solving a code in the Crazy Place, a challenge around the map (fill soul chests, shoot floating orbs, align the rings), then charging it on its Crazy Place pedestal. Upgraded staffs are the strongest weapons in all of BO2.
  • Build the Maxis Drone (parts around the site) — it revives teammates, kills zombies, and is required for the Easter egg. The G-Strike beacon calls in an airstrike that clears any train, anywhere.

Main Easter egg — Little Lost Girl

  1. Open the Crazy Place and build all four staffs. Upgrade all four — each has a Crazy Place puzzle, an open-world challenge, and a soul-charge on its pedestal.
  2. Place the upgraded staffs in their stands: three go inside the giant robots' feet (match staff to robot as the voices describe) and one in the Crazy Place.
  3. Throw a G-Strike at the glowing seal on the ground by the Excavation Site to crack it open, then melee the zombies pouring out with the elemental fists until the portal charge completes.
  4. Release the Maxis Drone into the open portal. Reward: the ending cutscene — Samantha is freed, and the Primis crew's story rolls straight into Black Ops 3.

Songs: “Archangel” by Elena Siegman, Kevin Sherwood & Malukah (three teddy bears) and Avenged Sevenfold's “Shepherd of Fire” as the intro theme.

NUKEBonus Map

Nuketown Zombies

Nuketown 2025, Nevada · The perks fall from the sky

Survival · No main questDifficulty: Medium

How it plays

The multiplayer classic, overrun. There are no perk machines on the ground at the start — as the population counter on the Nuketown sign ticks down with your kills, perk machines and eventually the Pack-a-Punch machine drop from the sky into random yards. Which perks you get, and when, is luck — a game might give you Juggernog at round 6 or round 16. The Mystery Box sits in one of the back yards; the houses' back gardens are your training loops.

Best strategy

  • Train in the cul-de-sac and back yards — the map is small, so keep the loop wide and never get pinned in a house.
  • Shoot the heads off the mannequins for the map's hidden musical easter egg flavor, and check the sign counter to know how close the next drop is.
  • When a machine drops, buy Juggernog first no matter the round — drops are not guaranteed to come back.
  • At round 25 the counter hits zero and the drops speed up — the late game is a perk-rich sprint.
DEPOTLaunch · Green Run

Bus Depot — Survival

Green Run bus station · The stripped-down challenge

No Perks · No Pack-a-PunchDifficulty: Hard (by design)

How it plays

Just the TranZit starting area as a standalone survival map: the depot interior, the lava-cracked parking lot, the box, and wall guns. There are no perk machines and no Pack-a-Punch here — the BO2 answer to Nacht der Untoten. The Turbine workbench is here, so you can still build and carry a Turbine out the door.

Best strategy

  • Hold the depot's back room early, then move outside and train wide around the bus before the lava lanes funnel you.
  • The MP5 and Olympia off the wall, plus box luck, are your whole arsenal — conserve ammo, knife the first rounds clean.
  • Round 15+ with no Juggernog is the real test — one mistake ends it, so keep a crawler before every box spin.
FARMLaunch · Green Run

Farm — Survival & Grief

Green Run farmstead · Barn, house, and open ground

Perks + Box · No Pack-a-PunchDifficulty: Medium

How it plays

The TranZit farm stop as its own map: barn, farmhouse, and the fields between. Perk machines and the Mystery Box are here, but no Pack-a-Punch — your guns stay stock, so ammo discipline and traps do the heavy lifting. Farm also hosts Grief mode: two teams of four share the map, can't shoot each other, and win by being the last team standing — grief the other team's revives and box spins at your own risk.

Best strategy

  • The barn loft is the classic holdout — one player on the stairs, the rest covering the windows and the drop-down.
  • Open ground between the house and barn is your training loop once the barn gets hot.
  • In Grief, a downed enemy you don't revive is worth more than any kill — body-block their teammates' revive attempts.
TOWNLaunch · Green Run

Town — Survival & Grief

Green Run town center · Lava streets, full perk set

Perks + Pack-a-PunchDifficulty: Medium

How it plays

The biggest of the Green Run survival maps and the most complete: the full perk set, the Mystery Box, and Pack-a-Punch inside the bank vault (blow the doors with explosives, same as TranZit). Lava cracks split the streets, so routes matter. Town also runs Grief mode for two teams of four.

Best strategy

  • Train the big loop around the bar and the bank — wide streets, two exits everywhere, and the lava lanes funnel zombies into lines.
  • Juggernog is in the bar area; Stamin-Up makes the lava jumps painless and is a legit second buy here.
  • The vault is a deathtrap as a camp spot — upgrade and get out.
Best of the three:  If you only play one Green Run survival map, make it Town — it's the only one with perks and Pack-a-Punch, and it's where most BO2 high-round survival records were set.

High-Round Rules That Work on Every Map

Build first, train second

BO2 high rounds are won at the workbench. Get the Zombie Shield built, your map's crowd-control buildable running (Trample Steam, Resonator, Turbine traps), and your wonder weapon upgraded before you settle into a training loop. Past round 30, run a loop with two exits, let the whole wave spawn in, then turn and clear the train with your strongest gun — upgraded staffs, the Sliquifier, and the Acid Gat exist precisely for that turn-and-clear moment.

Downs, bosses & recovery

  • Brutus and the Panzer Soldat get more frequent, not less — keep a dedicated boss plan (Electric Cherry reloads, a stored G-Strike, a Time Bomb snapshot) instead of improvising.
  • After a revive: Juggernog first, then shield, then perks. On TranZit, Die Rise, and Buried, your banked points make recovery cheap — bank when you're rich, not when you're desperate.
  • Call out drops before touching them. A Max Ammo grabbed before reloads, or a Carpenter during a boss wave, loses more rounds than any zombie.
  • Save a crawler before Easter egg steps, plane trips, and robot-foot rides — every BO2 quest step assumes you have time.

Quick Answers

Which BO2 map should a beginner start on?

Mob of the Dead. It teaches Afterlife, buildables, and wonder-weapon side quests on a fair map with a forgiving training loop by the docks and cafeteria. Then Buried — Leroy and the Paralyzer make it the friendliest of the big quest maps. Save Origins for last.

Maxis or Richtofen — which side should I pick?

Whichever your regular crew picks — the choice carries across TranZit, Die Rise, and Buried, and mixing sides mid-story blocks steps. Mechanically the endings differ in Buried (Mined Games resolves differently per side), but neither side is harder overall. First-time crews usually run Maxis: the TranZit Maxis path is the simpler of the two.

What is the best wonder weapon in BO2?

The upgraded Ice Staff and Fire Staff on Origins are the strongest weapons in the game, full stop. Outside Origins: the Acid Gat / Magma Gat on Mob of the Dead and the Sliquifier on Die Rise carry high rounds, and the Paralyzer on Buried is the best pure crowd-control tool — it never needs ammo.

How do the bank and weapon locker work?

On TranZit, Die Rise, and Buried, the bank lets you deposit points (in 1,000-point chunks) that persist between games on those maps — withdraw them in a later game for a fast start, minus a small fee; all three maps share the one account. The weapon locker/fridge stores one gun when you interact with it, and you can pull that same gun out in a future game on any of the three maps. Store something Pack-a-Punched.
