Start Here — The BO1 Basics

BO1 runs on the classic points economy. Everything below applies to every map in this guide unless a map card says otherwise.

Points & the early rounds

  • Round 1–3: Shoot a zombie 5–6 times in the chest, then knife it. That is worth far more than headshots early and funds your first doors.
  • Repair windows between rounds — 10 points per board, free points while zombies are held off by the boards.
  • Don't open everything. Open one route to power, buy Juggernog, then open the rest. Every open door is another spawn window to watch.
  • Save a crawler at the end of a round whenever you need to hit the box, link teleporters, or do Easter egg steps.

Mystery Box & Pack-a-Punch

  • Box: 950 points per spin. The teddy bear moves the box to a new location — follow the light beam in the sky.
  • Pack-a-Punch: 5,000 points. Full ammo refill on the upgraded gun, more damage, bigger mag, and a new name and camo. Keep one wall gun you can re-buy ammo for instead of depending on the box twice.
  • Max Ammo drops refill both weapons — call it out and reload before anyone picks it up.
  • Fire Sale (on maps that have it) drops every box location to 10 points — spin fast, it only lasts 30 seconds.

The Maps

In release order. Use the filters above to jump to launch maps, DLC, or the World at War classics bundled in Rezurrection.

KINOLaunch

Kino der Toten

Abandoned Nazi theater, Berlin · The starter map

Wonder Weapon: ThundergunSpecial: Nova CrawlersDifficulty: Easy–Medium

Power & Pack-a-Punch

Power switch is on the stage — take either route from the lobby (upstairs through the dressing room or downstairs through the kitchen side). Once power is on, stand on the teleporter pad in the lobby and link it to the mainframe at the Pack-a-Punch projector room. Each teleport sends you to the projector room for a short window to use Pack-a-Punch before you are dropped back — and a random Samantha room or perk room can appear on the way.

Best strategy

  • Train on the stage once power is on — the widest open loop on the map, with the trap and curtain spawns easy to read.
  • Alley is a strong early holdout until about round 12.
  • Thundergun + a light machine gun or upgraded MP40 off the wall covers almost everything.
  • Watch the Nova Crawlers after teleporting — they explode into gas; shoot them, don't knife them.

Musical Easter egg — “115” by Elena Siegman

  1. Find and activate three meteor fragments (hold the interact button until you hear the cue): one in the lobby corner by the stairs, one in the dressing room on a shelf/pedestal, and one in the room past the stage near the window.
  2. Activate all three and the song plays. There is no full quest Easter egg on Kino — the meteor pieces and the film reels that appear in the projector-room trips are the story layer here.
FIVELaunch

“Five”

The Pentagon · JFK, Nixon, Castro & McNamara

Wonder Weapon: Winter's HowlBoss: Pentagon ThiefDifficulty: Hard

Power & Pack-a-Punch

Power is in the basement labs — ride the elevator down from the War Room side. Pack-a-Punch is in the War Room but locked behind DEFCON 5: flip all five DEFCON switches around the War Room to lower DEFCON, then step through the portal. The DEFCON level counts back up over time, so move as a team.

The Pentagon Thief

  • Appears every few rounds after power, only one player can see him at first — he steals the weapon of whoever he touches.
  • Kill him before he escapes and you get your gun back plus a Bonfire Sale drop (Pack-a-Punch drops to 1,000 points) or a Max Ammo.
  • Winter's Howl freezes groups solid — one of the few guns that makes the tight War Room survivable late.

Musical Easter egg — “Won't Back Down” by Eminem

  1. Activate three red telephones: one in the starting office area, one in the War Room, and one in the Pack-a-Punch room area.
  2. All three phones ring the song in. Five has no multi-step quest egg — the Thief, DEFCON system, and the televisions showing the outbreak are its signature systems.
ASCDLC 1 · First Strike

Ascension

Soviet cosmodrome, Kazakhstan · First big quest map

Wonder Weapons: Thundergun + Gersh DeviceSpecial: Space MonkeysDifficulty: Medium

Power & Pack-a-Punch

Power is at the top of the facility past the centrifuge room. Pack-a-Punch unlocks by riding all three Lunar Landers — ride each lander back to the launch platform (each ride costs 250), and once all three have been used, the rocket launch button by the power area opens the Pack-a-Punch room under the rocket. Launch the rocket for a Double Points drop.

Space Monkeys — read this

  • On perk-machine rounds, monkeys drop in and attack your perk machines. If they damage one enough, you lose that perk.
  • Defend the machines — don't open areas whose perks you can't defend. Killing every monkey without a machine taking damage earns a random free perk (the only way to hold more than 4).
  • New perks Stamin-Up and PHD Flopper debut here.

Main Easter egg — Casimir Mechanism (co-op recommended)

  1. Use the four Gersh Device / Matryoshka steps in order: free the mechanism by throwing a Gersh Device at the sparking generator outside the map near the PHD Flopper area.
  2. Press the four glowing buttons that appear around the map within the time limit (all players at once in co-op).
  3. Stand in the centrifuge / complete the pressure and letter steps: ride the landers to spell and hold the nodes as prompted by the voices.
  4. Throw a Gersh Device at the final orb and empty upgraded weapons into it to free Gersh — the reward is a Death Machine for 90 seconds for the whole team. Note: unlike later maps, Ascension's egg gives no permanent perks.

Song: “Abracadavre” by Elena Siegman — activate the three teddy bears holding sickles around the map.

COTDDLC 2 · Escalation

Call of the Dead

Frozen Siberian shipwreck & lighthouse · George Romero boss

Wonder Weapons: V-R11 + ScavengerBoss: George A. RomeroDifficulty: Hard

Power & Pack-a-Punch

Power is on the ship's upper deck — clear the wreck debris from spawn and work up the ship. Pack-a-Punch appears at the top of the lighthouse on a rotating/random cycle with the lighthouse beam — watch where the light points; it also periodically moves. Ziplines and the Flinger get you around fast, but the ice water slows you and spawns crawlers — stay out of it on high rounds.

George Romero

  • George is always on the map, slowly following you with a stage light. Shooting or exploding him enrages him — he sprints and swings.
  • Calm him by leading him into deep water.
  • Killing him (it takes a huge amount of damage, Scavenger and V-R11 combos work best) drops a Death Machine and a free random perk, and he returns a few rounds later.
  • The V-R11 turns zombies back into humans who sprint away distracting the horde — and shooting a teammate with the upgraded V-R11 gives them a brief invulnerability aura.

Main Easter egg — Ensemble Cast / Stand-In

  1. Free the original crew trapped behind the vault door in the lighthouse's lower level — get the fuse from the rooms above (kill a zombie near the door area drops it / it spawns in the nearby cabins) and install it in the panel by the door.
  2. Destroy the four vodka bottles the crew asks for by knifing/shooting them around the map.
  3. Complete the lighthouse dials puzzle: set the four colored dials in the lighthouse stairwell to the correct combination (the crew calls out the order).
  4. Use the ship's wheel and controls as directed, then recover the Golden Rod with a V-R11-charged zombie in the final step — deliver it to the door. Reward: the Wunderwaffe DG-2 for the player who completes it (and Lightning Bolt drops).

Song: “Not Ready to Die” by Avenged Sevenfold — activate the three meteor fragments.

SHANGDLC 3 · Annihilation

Shangri-La

Lost jungle temple · The hardest BO1 egg

Wonder Weapon: 31-79 JGb215 (Baby Gun)Specials: Napalm + ShriekerDifficulty: Very Hard

Power & Pack-a-Punch

Two power switches — one on each side of the temple (the mud-room side and the mine side); both must be flipped. Pack-a-Punch is earned: stand on the pressure plates at spawn as a full team (solo players get a timed window), which raises the stairs to the altar at the top of the temple. Miss the window and the stairs drop — do your upgrading in one trip.

Survival notes

  • The map is tight — train in the open spawn area or the AK-74u room, never in the tunnels.
  • Napalm zombie explodes when close — kill it at range. Shrieker sprints and blurs your screen — kill it first.
  • Monkeys steal drops and cycle them — shoot the monkey to lock in the drop you want.
  • The Baby Gun shrinks zombies so you can run through and kick them — upgraded, it's one of the strongest panic buttons in BO1.

Main Easter egg — Time Travel Will Tell (4 players required)

  1. Activate Eclipse mode using the eclipse buttons at spawn (all four players press simultaneously).
  2. Work through the eclipse steps in order: charge the plates, use the spikemores and the Baby Gun on the shrunken targets, complete the water-slide and mud-pressure steps, and place the Focusing Stone components as Richtofen directs.
  3. Repeat the sequence across past/present eclipse trips — twelve main steps in total, several are timed and several need specific box weapons (dynamite, the shrink gun, and a sniper for the final shots).
  4. Final reward: all seven perks permanently for Richtofen's player (the Focusing Stone) — the strongest single reward in BO1. This egg cannot be finished solo or with fewer than four players.

Song: “Pareidolia” by Elena Siegman — activate the three meteor fragments.

MOONDLC 4 · Rezurrection

Moon

Griffin Station, the Moon + Area 51 · The BO1 finale

Wonder Weapons: Wave Gun + QEDSpecial: Astronaut (steals a perk)Difficulty: Very Hard

Start, power & Pack-a-Punch

You start in No Man's Land (Area 51) — survive the sprint for points, grab Juggernog or Speed Cola if they are up, then take the teleporter before you are overrun. On the Moon: you need the P.E.S. suit to breathe outside. Power is in the labs area. Pack-a-Punch stays in Area 51 — teleport back during a calm round, upgrade fast, and return; No Man's Land gets harder every visit. The Hacker device (found in the labs, replaces your P.E.S. slot choice) hacks perks, doors, the box, and downed teammates cheaper or faster — and hacks Pack-a-Punch for a chance at better outcomes.

Survival notes

  • The Astronaut zombie is slow and tanky — if he grabs you he teleports you and steals a random perk. Kill him at range; he explodes, so keep distance.
  • Excavators can breach the biodome and tunnels and cut off oxygen — use the hacker on the excavator controls in Receiving Bay to stop them.
  • Train in the Biodome — low gravity jumps make it the best loop in BO1. Bounce pads save ammo and lives.
  • The Wave Gun splits into dual Zap Guns; the combined Wave Gun microwave blast clears a whole train.

Main Easter egg — Richtofen's Grand Scheme / Big Bang Theory

  1. Power the pyramid: fill the four soul tubes by killing zombies near the M.P.D. pyramid in Receiving Bay until each tube is full.
  2. Use the Hacker and the QED through the mid steps: hack the four control panels in the labs within the time window, and throw a QED at the excavator / plates as directed to move the plates into place.
  3. Collect the Golden Rod / Vril Generator from the earlier maps' storyline items, place it, and complete Samantha's games: play the “Samantha Says” computer sequence in the labs correctly.
  4. Charge the final orbs with the Wave Gun and Gersh Devices, swap souls at the pyramid (in co-op, the Richtofen player completes the swap), then throw a QED at the final device — the cutscene plays and the Earth is hit by three rockets.
  5. Reward: all eight perks permanently (Big Bang Theory achievement version) — you keep them even after going down, for the rest of the match.

Songs: “Coming Home” by Elena Siegman (three teddy bears) and the hidden “Nightmare” by Avenged Sevenfold after completing the egg.

NACHTRezurrection Classic

Nacht der Untoten

Ruined bunker · Where Zombies began (WaW remaster)

No Perks · No Pack-a-PunchDifficulty: Hard (by design)

How it plays

The original three-room map, exactly as it was: a starting room, the Help room with the Mystery Box, and the upstairs with the sniper cabinet. There is no power, no perks, and no Pack-a-Punch in the BO1 version — just wall guns, the box, grenades, and your aim.

Best strategy

  • Hold the starting room until you can afford the Help door, then camp the corner by the box with a wall LMG or the Thompson upstairs.
  • Upstairs grenade spam off the balcony is the classic high-round play — buy grenades every round.
  • Radio in the starting room plays messages when shot/knifed — pure story flavor.
Why play it:  Nacht is the purest form of the mode. If you can hit round 20 here with no perks, every other map in this guide feels generous.
VERRRezurrection Classic

Verrückt

Wittenau asylum, Berlin · Split-start co-op (WaW remaster)

First map with perks & powerDifficulty: Medium–Hard

Power & layout

The team starts split in two halves of the asylum. Power — in the generator room between the two sides — reunites the team and turns on the perk machines and the electro-shock defenses. No Pack-a-Punch on this classic version. The corridors are narrow: this map teaches spawn control better than any other.

Best strategy

  • Open toward power first, not toward more rooms.
  • The balcony by Speed Cola is the classic camp spot — one player watches the stairs, one the window.
  • Song: “Lullaby for a Dead Man” — the series' first musical egg originated here.
SHIRezurrection Classic

Shi No Numa

Tunguska swamp · Hellhounds' debut (WaW remaster)

Wonder Weapon: Wunderwaffe DG-2Special: HellhoundsDifficulty: Medium

Layout & perks

No power switch — instead, four outer huts each hold one random perk machine, so perk locations change every game. Open the hut doors to activate them. The Flogger trap and the zipline from the Doctor's Quarters are your crowd control. Pack-a-Punch is not on this classic version.

Best strategy

  • Hellhound rounds start around round 5–7 — finish the last hound and a Max Ammo drops. Save it until you've reloaded.
  • The Wunderwaffe chains lightning through a whole train — don't waste it on single zombies.
  • Train around the main hut's center loop; the swamp water slows you, so stay on the paths and bridges.
RIESERezurrection Classic

Der Riese

Group 935 factory, Germany · The Pack-a-Punch debut (WaW remaster)

Wonder Weapon: Wunderwaffe DG-2First Pack-a-Punch mapDifficulty: Medium

Power & Pack-a-Punch

Power is at the back of the factory past the courtyard. Pack-a-Punch works the way Kino copied: link all three teleporters (A, B, and C) to the mainframe pad at spawn — each link costs a short trip and the pad charges between uses. Once all three are linked, the Pack-a-Punch vault at spawn opens permanently.

Best strategy & secrets

  • The catwalk above the furnace room is the most famous camp spot in Zombies history — hold the stairs, watch the window.
  • The Fly Trap: shoot the hidden panel outside the map with an upgraded weapon to start Samantha's hide-and-seek — find the hidden teddy bears and monkey for the “Acep in the Hole” reward and story audio.
  • Hellhounds appear here too — the upgraded Wunderwaffe and the catwalk make Der Riese the best high-round teacher in the classics set.
DOALaunch · Hidden Mode

Dead Ops Arcade

Top-down twin-stick arena · The hidden arcade mode

Unlock: computer terminal codeDifficulty: Medium

How to unlock it

At the main menu, break out of the chair (look down and mash the triggers), walk to the computer terminal behind you, and type DOA — or type 3ARC UNLOCK to unlock Dead Ops and “Five” together.

How it plays

  • 40 rounds of arena survival across themed islands, ending with the Cosmic Silverback boss.
  • Grab gems and treasure for extra lives; the Fate system after the first boss fight grants a permanent bonus (like the chicken companion or extra speed).
  • Golden items stack — prioritize the boots, the chicken, and weapon-duration pickups.
No map matches that search — try a map name, a wonder weapon, or a perk.

High-Round Rules That Work on Every Map

Training, not camping

Past round 25, camping fails everywhere except a few classic spots. Run a loop with at least two exits, let the whole wave spawn in, then turn and clear the train with your strongest gun. A tight train is one Thundergun shell, one Wave Gun shot, or one Wunderwaffe bolt — that ammo math is how high rounds are won.

Downs & recovery

  • Keep a crawler before doing anything risky.
  • After a revive: Juggernog first, then your main gun's ammo, then the rest of your perks.
  • Call out drops before touching them — a wasted Max Ammo or an Insta-Kill grabbed at the wrong second loses more rounds than any zombie.
  • Monkey Bombs (from the box) buy 8–10 seconds of total safety — save them for revives and Pack-a-Punch trips, not for fun.

Quick Answers

Which BO1 map should a beginner start on?

Kino der Toten. It teaches power, teleporters, Pack-a-Punch, and training on the stage without any punishing extra systems. Move to Ascension next, then Der Riese from the classics.

Which Easter eggs can be done solo?

Call of the Dead's egg has a dedicated solo version (Stand-In). Moon's egg can be completed solo in the BO1 version, though the final steps are far easier with help. Shangri-La's Time Travel Will Tell strictly requires four players. Ascension's egg is possible solo but the button step is extremely tight alone.

What is the best wonder weapon in BO1?

For raw train-clearing, the Thundergun (Kino/Ascension) and the Wave Gun (Moon) are the top tier. The Wunderwaffe DG-2 on Shi No Numa and Der Riese chains through ten zombies per shot and never really falls off. The Baby Gun on Shangri-La is the best panic button because shrunk zombies can simply be run over.

How do I unlock “Five” and Dead Ops Arcade without playing the campaign?

Break out of the menu chair, use the computer terminal, and type 3ARC UNLOCK. That unlocks “Five”, Dead Ops Arcade, and the campaign missions. Typing DOA alone unlocks just Dead Ops Arcade.
