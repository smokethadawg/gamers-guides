The Tomb is two loops that meet at the Subterranean Temple. North path: Dig Site → Tombs → Shrine of the Hierophants → Temple. South path: Dig Site → Neolithic Catacombs → Ossuary → Temple. Place the amulet in the Temple to open the Doorway to Nowhere — the red portal into the Dark Aether Nexus, where Pack-a-Punch starts. After that, four coloured portals (blue = Dig Site, yellow = Shrine, green = Ossuary, red = Temple) make the Nexus your fast-travel hub. Pack-a-Punch later swaps between the Nexus and the Dig Site, so check the stone maps around the map.

Dark Aether Nexus — through the Doorway to Nowhere

Dark Aether Nexus

Central mound & altar where the Staff of Ice is built and upgraded. Four trial statues ring the altar. Portals: 🔵 Dig Site · 🟡 Shrine · 🟢 Ossuary · 🔴 Temple.

Tip: once the Nexus is open you never need to run the full loop again — portal in from any zone, cross the Nexus, portal out the other side. It's the fastest route to PaP, Jugger-Nog and Wunderfizz from anywhere.

The No-Miss Route

One run, everything in order

Follow this order and you'll touch every system on the map without backtracking. Pick the north path on your first runs — Death Perception early makes the dig-pile egg much easier, and both paths cost exactly the same (2,250 + 2,500 + 2,750).

Rounds
1–3

Farm the Dig Site. Knife and headshot for Essence, don't buy the Marine SP yet. Break purple Aether Crystals for salvage. Grab the Infamous Mr. Rhodes and Retirement Plan Intel at spawn (see Intel list) and buy the Level II Armor Vest if you're staying a third round.

Round
4

Open door 1 (2,250) into the Tombs. Pick up Grave Robbers Intel right inside the door. Note the bull mural and the Death Perception machine — you'll be back here with a lantern flame and 3 gems.

Round
5

Open door 2 (2,500) into the Shrine of the Hierophants. Grab headset #1 by the statue, the Spring Time and Against a Wall Intel, and shoot the hidden Full Power in the spawn cave right of Stamin-Up. Mind the Arrow Traps on the stairs.

Rounds
6–8

Open door 3 (2,750) into the Subterranean Temple. Take The Knaves Intel by the altar stairs, place the amulet on the pedestal, and step through the Doorway to Nowhere. In the Nexus: Pack-a-Punch Tier 1, Jugger-Nog, Bernard's skull Intel on the central podium, and the Tortured Artist Intel by the green portal ruins.

Round
8

Kill the first Shock Mimic — do not let it despawn. It drops Archibald's Monocle, the key that makes the constellation symbols visible. No Monocle, no Staff. Then shoot the Nexus Double Points and Bonus Points (in that order) and headset #3 between the yellow and red portals.

Rounds
9–12

Build the Staff of Ice (full steps below): lantern flame to the bull mural → Taurus lockdown → Staff piece; lantern flame to the archer mural → Orion lockdown → Head Piece; build and defend it on the Nexus altar. On the way through the south loop, open the Catacombs and Ossuary, grab Speed Cola, headset #2, and light the 6 Ossuary bowls for a free Random Perk.

Rounds
12–15

Free-loot sweep while the Staff is fresh: Dead Wire the Dig Site generators, freeze the Nexus waterfalls, shoot the floating rocks, dig for the 3 Ancient Gems with Death Perception, and run the Gold Armor blood sacrifices. Then upgrade the Staff to Ull's Arrow — freeze 3 crystals in 10 seconds, floating-rock symbols, orb escort.

Rounds
15–20

Four statue trials in quest order: Vermin → Parasite → Doppelghast → Amalgam. Carry each amulet energy carefully — getting hit 3 times while carrying it fails the carry and you restart that leg. Refill Sekhmet's Vigor ammo at any Ammo Cache (5,000) if you run dry.

Before
Boss

Full prep: Tier 3 PaP + Legendary rarity on a bullet gun (ASG-89, GS45 akimbo or a strong AR/LMG build), Tier 3 armor or Gold Armor, 4+ perks incl. Jugger-Nog, Speed Cola, Stamin-Up, Quick Revive, Aether Shroud or Frenzied Guard, Kazimirs/Monkey Bombs, a Self-Revive and your Staff for crowd control only. Then take the north-east bridge in the Nexus.

Main Quest — The Sentinel Artifact

0 / 11

Rewards: the X91 Ice-Pike Staff of Ice skin, "The Tomb Quest Complete" calling card and 5,000 XP (Standard mode before the Directed-mode cutoff also earns the early-completion calling card variant). Tick each step as you clear it.

Boss damage rule: the Sentinel Artifact only takes damage from bullet weapons, and only while it glows ghostly purple/blue. Explosives, the Ray Gun and the Staff of Ice itself do not damage it — the Staff is for clearing the horde at your feet, your PaP'd bullet gun is for the boss.

Staff of Ice — Build & Upgrade

0 / 9

The map's Wonder Weapon, straight out of Origins. Primary fire freezes and shatters; the upgraded Ull's Arrow adds a charged ice storm and Sekhmet's Vigor — a green revive shot that also wakes the trial statues. In co-op, once one player upgrades, teammates can take a crystal shard by the monolith to upgrade their own staffs.

Hidden & Free Power-Ups

0 / 9

Eight power-ups are hidden in the scenery from the start of the game — shoot one to teleport it to you — and a ninth free power-up, the Random Perk, is earned from the Ossuary fire-bowl egg (some trackers count 9 free power-ups on this map for exactly that reason; it's also listed in Side Eggs). Of the hidden eight, Fire Sale only spawns after you've collected the other seven, so work the hidden list top to bottom. Save Max Ammo and Insta-Kill for when you actually need them; they stay put until shot.

Boss Fight — The Sentinel Artifact

One arena, nine phases, zero cover from the laser except the arena's two levels. The Artifact sweeps a red beam that stuns and nearly kills — when it targets the top floor, drop to the bottom; when it targets the bottom, climb. A Max Ammo spawns under the Artifact at the start of every new phase.

Go in with

  • Bullet gun, Tier 3 PaP, Legendary rarity — the only thing that hurts the boss. ASG-89, akimbo GS45s, or a high-DPS AR/LMG.
  • Staff of Ice (Ull's Arrow) — crowd control only. Freeze the horde, never waste it on the Artifact.
  • Tier 3 armor or Gold Armor, full plates in reserve.
  • Jugger-Nog, Quick Revive, Speed Cola, Stamin-Up minimum; Deadshot pays off in DPS phases.
  • Aether Shroud for the final DPS check, Kazimirs or Monkey Bombs for breathing room, a Self-Revive each.

How the phases run

  • Phases 1–4 — split: dodge the spinning laser, kill adds. When the Artifact stops, rises and turns ghostly purple, mag-dump it. Each damage chunk costs it a piece of health, drops a Max Ammo, and splits off another Artifact — up to 4 spinning at once.
  • Phases 5–8 — hosts: an Artifact latches onto an elite — a heavy zombie, then Doppelghast, Shock Mimic, and finally an Amalgam. Kill the host while the other Artifacts are still spinning (not purple), then shoot the dropped Artifact until it breaks before it floats back to its pedestal.
  • Phase 9 — the DPS check: the Artifacts form a semi-circle and one mounts the north-east wall. This is a hard damage race: if you don't break it in time, the room wipes the whole squad. Pop Aether Shroud, stand your ground, and burn it down.
  • Finish: interact with the Artifact on the pedestal to end the quest and roll the cutscene.

Solo players: this boss is widely rated the hardest of BO6's first four maps to solo, purely because of the phase-9 DPS check. If your gun can't melt an HVT in one magazine by round 25, upgrade rarity and PaP before you raise the bridge — there's no leaving once it starts.
